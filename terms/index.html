1. What HabitToll is

HabitToll blocks the apps you want to use less, using Apple's Screen Time. When you open a blocked app, a block screen appears. You can:

  • walk away ("Not worth it");
  • use one of the free ways through (section 4); or
  • buy a 10-minute unlock at a price you chose yourself.

Blocking, your statistics, the monthly bill, streaks and Friends cost nothing. There's no subscription.

HabitToll is a self-control tool. It isn't a medical product or a treatment, and we can't promise any particular result.

2. Who can use it

You must be at least 18. You use HabitToll on your own device, for yourself. Don't use it to control someone else's device.

3. Unlocks you pay for

  • You set the rules. During setup and in Settings, you choose:
    • your price per unlock;
    • whether the price doubles for each further unlock the same day. It goes up to at most 10× your price, and resets at 04:00;
    • a monthly limit. Prices above €19.99 need one.
  • What one unlock is: a one-time in-app purchase, a "10-minute pass". It opens the app you tapped for 10 minutes of use. It starts right away, and ends after 10 minutes of use or 60 minutes after it started, whichever comes first. Unused minutes don't carry over.
  • You buy it from Apple. Every payment goes through Apple's purchase sheet, at the price Apple shows there, under Apple's Media Services Terms. Apple charges you and handles the payment.
  • The price before you pay: the block screen and the pay screen show the price of this unlock, and of the next one, before Apple's sheet opens.
  • Your monthly limit:
    • Past your limit, the app asks before every paid unlock.
    • With "Stop at my limit" on, paid unlocks stop until the 1st of the next month. Emergency unlocks still work.
    • A purchase you already confirmed on Apple's sheet, or one waiting for approval, is always delivered when it completes.
  • If a purchase completes late, for example after an approval, it becomes a prepaid unlock and is used for your next unlock.
  • If iOS ends a paid unlock early, before its 10 minutes of use, your next unlock is free.
  • Refunds are decided by Apple. You can ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, or through Help → Refunds in the app. Your legal rights as a consumer are not affected.

4. Free ways through

  • "Not worth it" is always one tap, and always free.
  • Depending on your settings, you also have:
    • free minutes each day, one pass shared by all your apps;
    • 2 free emergency unlocks a month (10 minutes each). They also work when your monthly limit is reached;
    • free make-up unlocks (section 3).
  • You can remove apps, lower your price or stop blocking in Settings. If you turned on the settings lock, these changes wait 24 hours or need your partner's code.
  • Switching off Screen Time access for HabitToll in the iPhone's Settings, or deleting the app, always ends blocking.

5. The settings lock

If you turn on the settings lock, changes that make blocking easier wait 24 hours, or need the code of a person you chose. This is your own choice, for your own benefit. It never affects the free ways through in section 4.

Invite only people you know. Don't try to guess invite codes, send floods of invites or misuse Friends in any other way. If someone does, we may remove their Friends profile.
