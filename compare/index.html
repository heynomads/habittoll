HabitToll compared.

How HabitToll differs from Screen Time, Digital Wellbeing and other app blockers, with facts from the stores and the apps' own pages, dated.

  • HabitToll vs Screen Time on iPhoneApple’s Screen Time and HabitToll both block apps on iPhone. Screen Time gives categories a daily allowance and a schedule, protected by a passcode; HabitToll asks “worth it?” and puts a price you set on 10 minutes.
  • HabitToll vs Digital Wellbeing on AndroidAndroid’s Digital Wellbeing and HabitToll both limit apps. Digital Wellbeing closes an app when its daily timer runs out; HabitToll asks “worth it?” each time and puts a price you set on 10 minutes.
  • HabitToll vs OpalOpal blocks apps with sessions, schedules and limits, with a free plan and an Opal Pro subscription; HabitToll asks “worth it?” and puts a price you set on 10 minutes. Prices and differences, checked October 2026.
  • HabitToll vs one secone sec puts a short pause, like a deep breath, in front of the apps you choose; HabitToll asks “worth it?” and puts a price you set on 10 minutes. Prices, how each one works, and who each suits, checked October 2026.
  • HabitToll vs PushscrollPushscroll trades exercise for app time; HabitToll asks “worth it?” and puts a price you set on 10 minutes. Prices, how each one works, and who each suits, checked October 2026.
  • HabitToll vs BrainrotBrainrot blocks apps with schedules and a brain avatar that decays as you scroll; HabitToll asks “worth it?” and puts a price you set on 10 minutes. Prices and differences, checked October 2026.
