Where your money goes.

Your unlocks pay for HabitToll. Here is where the money goes.

From every unlock

Taxes and the store's share come off first: VAT goes to the state, and Apple or Google keep their share for the store and the payment.

The rest goes to Comads OÜ, the small company in Tallinn, Estonia, that makes HabitToll. It pays for building the app, its servers and support, and ads that bring HabitToll to more people.

You decide what it costs

You pick the price of 10 minutes and a monthly limit. Walking away is free, two emergency unlocks a month are free, and the best days cost nothing.
