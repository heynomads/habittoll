# HabitToll

HabitToll is an app blocker for iPhone and Android, made by Comads OÜ (Tallinn, Estonia). The apps you pick ask "Worth it?" before they open. Leaving is one tap. Going in means buying 10 minutes at a price you set, from €0.29. No subscription, no ads, no account. For adults (18 and older), in English, German and Spanish.
